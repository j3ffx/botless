Botless is ready

You can close this tab and go back to YouTube. Here is what to expect.

Verdicts build up as you browse

Botless reads YouTube’s own “Made with AI” label on the videos you watch, and keeps a record for each channel. A channel needs a few videos before it gets a verdict, so don’t expect many badges in the first minutes. They come as you watch.

  • Probably AI Its videos you’ve seen are consistently labeled (2 or more, by default). A single labeled video is flagged on its own.
  • Inconclusive Not enough to decide yet, or mixed signals.
  • Probably Human Several videos and no AI label at all. Hidden by default: Botless only shows it if you ask for it in Settings.

Many creators never disclose AI use, so a missing label is only weak evidence. Your own marks always win: mark any channel as AI or human from the toolbar popup.

Want verdicts sooner?

Turn on Active mode in the toolbar popup. Botless then checks the videos on your screen with YouTube before you watch them, without your account (no cookies) and a few seconds apart. It’s off by default: with it off, Botless sends nothing anywhere.

Tip: pin Botless to the toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the pin next to Botless.

Make it yours

Badge, fade or hide each verdict’s videos, or skip “Probably AI” videos automatically.
